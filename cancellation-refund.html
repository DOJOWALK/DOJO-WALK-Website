Cancellation & Refunds

Draft information · Last updated: 19 October 2026

No final refund promise is being made here. The public website does not currently accept bookings or payments. The company must approve a service-specific cancellation and refund policy and have it reviewed for applicable consumer law before enabling transactions.

1. Current website status

This informational website does not currently create a booking, charge a payment method or process a refund. Do not send money to anyone claiming to accept a DOJO WALK booking unless an official, verified payment flow is announced.

2. Before bookings open

Before launch, the final policy must explain how customers can cancel a one-time walk, a recurring plan or a selected-date walk; the applicable time windows; any permitted charges; and how to request help. The policy must be visible before a customer confirms a booking.

3. Walker or company cancellation

The final policy must explain what happens if a walker cannot attend, a booking cannot be fulfilled, the customer is unavailable at pickup or an operational issue affects the service. Any replacement, rescheduling or refund options must reflect actual capabilities and applicable law.

4. Refund timing and method

If payments are introduced, the company must disclose eligibility, the refund method, expected processing time and how payment-provider delays are handled. Actual timeframes should be published only after the payment process is verified.

5. How to request assistance

Official support contact details are pending confirmation. Do not invent or rely on unofficial contact details. Add a verified support email or in-product support channel before launching bookings.

6. Policy approval

Insert the approved effective date, operating legal entity, customer support route and final terms only after review by the company and qualified legal counsel.
