Privacy Notice

Draft for review · Last updated: 19 October 2026

This page is a starter privacy notice for the public DOJO WALK website. It is not a final legal policy. The company must verify its legal identity, data flows, vendors, retention practices and contact details before publishing this notice as final.

Important: This public website is informational and does not currently provide a live booking, payment, account registration or partner application form. Do not submit personal, payment, identity-document or pet-health information through unofficial channels.

1. Information collected

The static website may receive standard technical information through its hosting provider, such as request logs, browser type, approximate network information and security events. The website should collect information through forms, analytics, cookies or other tools only after the company has selected and configured those services and updated this notice accordingly.

2. How information may be used

Technical information may be used to deliver the website, protect it against abuse, diagnose errors and maintain security. If customer bookings, partner onboarding, support forms or analytics are introduced, the company must clearly explain the information collected and the specific purposes before those features go live.

3. Sharing and service providers

Website hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information to operate the site. Any additional sharing with payment providers, mapping services, communication vendors, analytics providers or operational partners must be documented before those integrations are enabled.

4. Retention and security

Retention periods and security measures must be defined according to the actual systems and applicable legal requirements. No website can promise absolute security. The company should document access controls, deletion procedures, backups and incident handling before launch.

5. Your choices and requests

Once official privacy contact details and operating entities are confirmed, add a verified way for people to ask about access, correction, deletion or other rights that may apply to them. Do not publish invented contact details.

6. Children

The intended service is for adult pet parents. The company should define and implement age-related controls in the relevant products and confirm the legal wording before launch.

7. Updates and contact

This notice must be updated when actual data practices change. Official company identity and privacy contact details are pending confirmation and must be inserted before this notice is treated as final.
