FAQs

Questions, answered clearly.

Key details about the planned DOJO WALK service.

Is booking open right now?

This public website does not currently provide a live booking form. Booking access should be announced once the customer flow and local operations are ready.

Which locations are supported?

Only areas enabled and staffed through the service-location and operations setup should be treated as supported. Confirm availability through the booking experience when it launches.

What walking options are planned?

The planned customer flow includes a one-time walk, a regular walking routine and walks on selected dates. Available options may vary by area.

How can I become a walking partner?

Partner onboarding is being developed separately. This page does not yet collect applications or documents.

Where can I get help or report a concern?

Official contact details and support channels need to be published before customer bookings open. Do not send sensitive pet or personal information through unofficial channels.

How are prices and cancellations handled?

Approved prices, plan conditions and cancellation terms should be presented before booking confirmation. This page does not make an unconfirmed price or refund promise.
